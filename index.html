Profile

Scientist and engineer with 12+ years of experience, united by a single thread: rainfall — measured, modelled, retrieved, and forecast across scales from street-level floods to continental climate systems.

That focus has demanded fluency across disciplines. Depending on the problem, I have worked as a hydrometeorologist designing stochastic storm models, a data scientist wrangling satellite and radar archives at HPC scale, an earth-observations specialist validating GPM and commercial microwave-link retrievals, a hydrologist building and training operational flood-forecasting pipelines, and a geospatial analyst applying geostatistics to reconstruct rainfall fields. I have shipped research code in Python, R, and Fortran, published in peer-reviewed journals, and presented at major international conferences — bridging the gap between science and engineering.

My breadth is deliberate, not accidental. Each role added a tool to the same toolkit; the result is a scientist who can go deep on the atmosphere, the data pipeline, or the model — and connect all three.
